# A Revenant

Awake? Your guide. Beside you, there's a tablet containing information about this era. If you have any questions, just ask me.

Your ears......are fox ears? Are them real?

Of course. Watch! They move with my will. By the way, I used to be humankind, but now I'm a demi-human.

"Used to be a human?" Isn't human experimentation forbidden? Where was it developed?

In fact, the demi-human nowadays is not gene-edited Homo sapiens. They are from other species. From furry to kemonomimi, the range is large. They are distinct from Homo sapiens and their original species, and form new species by themselves. The demi-human transformed from human, like me, is memory-copied body.

Memory-copied? Was it voluntary?

Of course I'm willing to. The Government strongly promote freedom, and they ban any form of coercion.

How about the original body? Do you feel uncomfortable when seeing another you?

......According to the SOP, the original one will be reshaped after being transformed. The transformed demi-human does not have the memory before the moment of transformation, so they don't know who they used to be. It stems from the "Memory Uniqueness" policy, which the Government guarantees: no memory may exist in duplicate at the same time."

Reshape? How? Melt down and rebuild? We're not made of metal.

Obviously not. That is for droids. Reshaping is reading out the memory, destroy the body, and write into new plain body in the Wall.

And I?

......The Government are executing the "Resurrection Project" to resurrect people died before.

Resurrection Project? Are you trying to resurrect old man? Am I somebody? Why you choose me?

To fill the lost population, the Government passed the Resurrection Project. The resurrection is ordered by time of death from latest to earliest. There are about one hundred thousand ancients resurrected today alone.

Which country is the Government, you keep mentioning? Is one of I know or a new one?

Yes and no. It was established by some of the countries you know, and it is the only supreme system now.

United into one? Those countries I know are too incompatible to merge into one. Which side lost the war? Or disintegrated?

Neither. After World War III, they realized that it's impossible to assimilate each other and avoid conflict. They built the Wall, named "the Wall of Freedom", to prevent any interaction or communication between them. It is as if the other does not exist in their world. The Government they jointly funded is only responsible for keeping peace and protecting human rights. It is the lowest common denominator for them.

The Wall of Freedom? You mentioned it again. That......that is crazy.
