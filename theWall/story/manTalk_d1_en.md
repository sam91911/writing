# A Revenant

Awake? Your guide. Beside you, there's a tablet containing information about this era. If you have any questions, just ask me.

Your ears......are fox ears? Is it real?

Of course. Watch! It moves with my will. By the way, I used to be humankind, but now I'm a humanoid.

"Used to be a human?" Isn't human experimentation forbidden? Where was it developed?

In fact, the humanoid nowadays is not gene-edited Homo sapiens. They are from other species. From furry to kemonomimi, the range is large. They are distinct from Homo sapiens and their original species, and form new species by themselves. The humanoid transformed from human, like me, is memory-copied body.

Memory-copied? Was it voluntary?

Of course I'm willing to. The Government strongly promote freedom, and they ban any form of coercion.

How about the original body? Do you feel uncomfortable when seeing another you?

......According to the SOP, the original bodies will be reformed after being transformed. The transformed humanoid does not have the memory before the moment of transformation, so they don't know who they used to be. It stems from the "Memory Uniqueness" policy, which the Government guarantees: no memory may exist in duplicate at the same time."
